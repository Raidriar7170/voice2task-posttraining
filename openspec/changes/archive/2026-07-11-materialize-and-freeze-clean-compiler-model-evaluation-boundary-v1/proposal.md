## Why

The archived clean-matched causal-evidence design is still `DESIGN_ONLY`: all 29 execution bindings are `UNBOUND_BY_DESIGN`, the protocol is not frozen, and neither independent evaluation partition exists. Until a new, lineage-clean acquisition boundary is bound, materialized, and sealed before outcome access, any compiler or model comparison would remain causally uninterpretable and risk reusing already-spent evidence.

## What Changes

- Add a fail-closed binding packet and validator that requires concrete, typed, source-hashed values for all 29 canonical execution bindings before acquisition can start; placeholders, aliases, conflicting values, and outcome-derived assumptions are rejected.
- Freeze a versioned protocol digest covering acquisition, family grouping, partition mechanics, compiler/model cards, power and uncertainty assumptions, guardrails, and stop rules before any clean row, gold label, prediction, or outcome is accessible.
- Materialize a private source-hashed acquisition frame into a family registry, run predeclared lineage/disjointness checks, assign every eligible semantic family exactly once to `compiler_system_evaluation` or `model_learning_evaluation`, and seal both memberships without opening either one-look partition.
- Keep private frame, family, and membership records outside git while publishing only sanitized aggregate manifests, hashes, counts, attestations, blocked reasons, and deterministic Human Brief/evidence navigation.
- Support two honest terminal paths: `POPULATION_MATERIALIZED_AND_SEALED` when every gate passes, or a public-safe blocked result when the source, bindings, lineage, size, privacy, or sealing requirements cannot be proven.
- Preserve `execution_readiness=false`, both causal-identification statuses blocked, arm artifacts unfrozen, and all compiler/model experiment, training, prediction, A100, one-look, data-tuning, and improvement-claim flags false.
- Do not implement a compiler/decoder, change prompts, decoding, schema, evaluator, or runtime policy, reuse lockbox-v1 rows, run generic chat fine-tuning, add skill routing or GUI action-policy learning, start GRPO, publish the full local corpus, release a checkpoint, or claim live-browser improvement.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `clean-compiler-model-evaluation-boundary`: bind and freeze the exact execution protocol, materialize a private metadata-only semantic-family registry, seal two deterministic family-disjoint partitions, and publish aggregate-only success or blocked evidence without opening either experiment.

### Modified Capabilities

None. The archived `clean-matched-causal-evidence-design` capability remains the immutable design input and is not rewritten by this operational materialization phase.

## Impact

- Adds a narrowly scoped materialization/freeze helper and CLI or script entry point, with typed binding, lineage, partition, sealing, and sanitization checks.
- Adds focused tests for exact binding coverage, source/hash drift, pre-outcome chronology, family disjointness, deterministic assignment, insufficient-size blocking, privacy boundaries, and idempotent replay.
- Uses the single canonical gitignored private input/output root `data/local-private/clean-compiler-model-evaluation-boundary-v1/`; no private path, row, family membership, gold, prediction, secret, or raw log enters committed evidence.
- Adds deterministic public-safe evidence only under `reports/public-sample/clean-compiler-model-evaluation-boundary-v1/`, updates current status/evidence navigation, and generates a concise Chinese Human Brief.
- Leaves model/compiler arm construction and every experiment for separately reviewed future changes.
