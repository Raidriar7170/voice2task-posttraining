## Why

The archived Contract Compiler V2 causal audit shows that current public dev/test are development-only and spent, lockbox-v1 is consumed one-look evidence, and neither compiler/system nor model-learning effects are causally identified. Before creating another evaluation set, implementing a compiler, or launching training, the project needs a preregistered design that preserves independent evidence for both estimands.

## What Changes

- Define one future clean acquisition and lineage plan that freezes family-level partition mechanics before acquisition, then assigns the materialized family registry into two family-disjoint sealed partitions before any gold or outcome access: one for a compiler/system one-look comparison and one reserved for a later model-learning one-look comparison.
- Define separate compiler and model-learning preregistration cards, observation units, eligible populations, interventions, controls, primary outcomes, full denominators, guardrails, invalid/unsupported handling, and attribution boundaries. The two estimands cannot share a headline effect.
- Predeclare one canonical execution-binding inventory, family-level partitioning, minimum-detectable-effect and sample-size methodology, paired within-record compiler contrasts with family-clustered uncertainty, at least three paired model seeds, model family/seed-level aggregation and uncertainty, all-assigned-seed failure handling, machine-checkable invariants, and negative controls.
- Define a fail-closed freeze/readiness state machine. This design phase ends at `DESIGN_ONLY`; it does not materialize or inspect clean rows and cannot make an experiment executable.
- During a later design-only apply, publish deterministic public-safe JSON/Markdown evidence plus a Chinese Human Brief with `clean_population_status=NOT_MATERIALIZED`, `experiment_preregistration_status=NOT_EXECUTABLE`, and `execution_readiness=false`.
- Preserve reproducibility of the archived causal audit when live `CONTEXT.md` advances by adding a content-addressed phase-time snapshot resolver. The archived report bytes, logical source path/hash, status, and metrics remain unchanged; the new design continues to bind the current live context.
- Recommend exactly one later materialization-and-freeze phase without executing it.
- Non-goals: no clean-row creation, selection, annotation, outcome access, data resplit, compiler/decoder implementation, prompt/schema/evaluator/runtime change, training, prediction, A100 execution, DPO/GRPO, lockbox-v1 raw-row reuse, archived-report rewrite, generic chat fine-tuning, skill routing, GUI action policy learning, checkpoint/adapter release, public full-corpus release, production/safety readiness claim, or live-browser benchmark claim.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `clean-matched-causal-evidence-design`: define the clean acquisition, sealed family-disjoint partition, compiler/system preregistration, model-learning preregistration, uncertainty, invariant, negative-control, freeze-state, and public-claim contracts required before any causal experiment can be materialized or executed.

### Modified Capabilities

None. This design consumes the archived `contract-compiler-v2-causal-audit`, `contract-evaluation`, dataset-boundary, and supervised-tuning truth surfaces without changing their requirements or runtime behavior.

## Impact

- New OpenSpec proposal/design/spec/tasks for a design-only causal evidence contract.
- A later apply may add a deterministic read-only design helper, focused tests, public-safe design reports under `reports/public-sample/`, navigation updates, and a Chinese Human Brief.
- Additive audit-only compatibility may add one content-addressed historical context snapshot and a logical-path resolver so the already archived audit remains byte-reproducible while current context evolves.
- No dataset, model, prediction, prompt, decoder, compiler, evaluator default, schema, execution path, A100 job, private artifact, or historical metric is changed by this proposal.
