## Claim Boundaries

Current evidence cannot claim overall model improvement from final SFT on lockbox v1, because strict exact match decreased versus base in the one-look aggregate comparison. It cannot claim executable quality improvement. It cannot claim production readiness. It cannot claim safety readiness. It cannot claim held-out recovery beyond the frozen lockbox aggregate metrics. It cannot claim live-browser benchmark gain. It cannot claim checkpoint release. It cannot claim adapter release. It cannot claim DPO justification. It cannot claim another canonical-candidate loop.

The final lockbox v1 result is one-look frozen aggregate evidence only: it used the frozen lockbox hash, pre-registered prompt policy, greedy decoding, schema guard, schema retry policy, strict evaluator, and the final SFT adapter hash. It must not be used to infer row-level failure causes, natural-ASR generalization, or an overall SFT causal effect, nor for row-level failure tuning, prompt tuning, schema changes, decoding changes, new training data, DPO/GRPO starts, or README headline claims without an explicit later review. Public dev/test are spent development evidence and cannot restore those claims. The Contract V2 projection is offline schema-burden evidence only: it removes derived/display-field burden from strict exact comparison, but it does not claim model improvement. The internal Contract V2 Core boundary now exists behind a V1-compatible deterministic envelope; it does not change the public V1 schema, V1 evaluator, training target, predictions, or downstream runtime. The Contract Compiler V2 causal audit defines mechanics and future matched-evidence requirements only: current compiler/system and model-learning identification remain blocked, and deterministic compiler-filled fields cannot be attributed to improved model parameters. The clean matched design preserves those blocked statuses, and the S0 materialization result confirms that no independent source or executable binding packet exists: neither surface is clean evidence, a frozen protocol, a materialized population, or a causal result. The slot mechanism analysis and hybrid slot representation design are read-only/design-only evidence. The copy-backed verification slice, shadow-mode integration, shadow interface review, prediction shadow hook, and recovered-adapter challenge run are provenance/interface evidence only. Challenge v1 is observed through recovered frozen adapters, but it remains a verifier adversarial fixture, not naturalistic generalization or task correctness evidence. Source-backed provenance is not task correctness, slot accuracy, executable quality, runtime enforcement, production readiness, or challenge-set generalization.

`READY_FOR_EXTERNAL_COMPLETION` is review-pack readiness only. It is not candidate review or acceptance, binding authority, protocol freeze, clean-population materialization, experiment eligibility, or execution authorization; the preparation-only pack does not supersede the archived S0 blocker.
